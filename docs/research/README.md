# Research Papers

[← Back to Engineering Rules](../../README.md)

This directory contains long-form research papers and conceptual work that sit beside, rather than inside, the engineering apprenticeship.

These papers are evidence-oriented publications. They should distinguish empirical findings from interpretation, state methodological limits, preserve citation provenance, and avoid presenting working papers as peer-reviewed research.

## Papers

1. **[The Angry Black Man: How a Racialized Threat Trope Shapes Perception, Punishment, Work, and Well-Being in the United States](angry-black-man.md)**  
   DJ Martin, 2026. Integrative scholarly review and conceptual paper. Introduces the proposed **Racialized Anger-Threat Loop (RATL)** and synthesizes evidence across childhood adultification, emotion perception, perceived physical formidability, education, work, policing, pain perception, and mental health.

## Reader Path

Start with the paper above. Its final section contains publication-development work required before formal journal submission, including reproducible literature-search methods, an evidence table, citation verification, and target-journal formatting.
